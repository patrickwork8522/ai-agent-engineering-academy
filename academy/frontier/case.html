Learn how to learn

How would you investigate this without Agent Atlas?

Step 1 · Find a primary source

Start with the official paper, product documentation, benchmark specification or incident postmortem. Read the publication date, evidence, methodology and explicit limitations—not just the headline.

Step 2 · Reproduce one mechanism

Use an official sample, notebook or open-source example, or make a tiny synthetic experiment. Change a single variable and observe the consequence.

Step 3 · Seek a counterexample

Ask what would make the proposed explanation false. Search for known failures, conflicting measurements and edge cases. Compare at least two sources with different incentives.

Step 4 · Explain and transfer

Write a plain-language explanation, a worked example, a test, and a professional decision memo. Revisit it later without looking at the solution.

Source evaluation checklist

  • Who produced it? Research team, official maintainers, independent audit or reseller?
  • What exactly was measured? Dataset, baseline, task definition, sample size, compute and environment?
  • Can you inspect the evidence? Code, logs, model cards, dataset or reproducible examples?
  • When was it valid? Libraries, model behavior and threat patterns can change quickly.
  • What is missing? Limitations, competing claims, commercial incentive and deployment conditions?

Try finding a second source independently, then compare its conclusion with the sources provided here.
